- Append each substantive user prompt to docs/prompt-log.md, preserving its wording. Avoid copying credentials or secrets; mark redactions.

## Highest-priority research requirement

All data and external resources must be cited and used ethically. Maintain docs/sources-and-ethics.md. Verify provenance, versions, applicable licenses and required attribution before incorporating data, code or models. Public access does not establish permission. Resolve unclear or conflicting terms for the affected use before proceeding. Cite original datasets and associated papers separately when both are used; attribute adapted code and methods. Preserve raw files and trace derived data, labels, splits and results to their sources and processing steps. Never fabricate citations or results. Distinguish measured, derived, simulated and assumed values. Record material AI assistance and follow institution/venue disclosure requirements. Respect privacy and access restrictions.
